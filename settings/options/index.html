Profile pictures

Show a picture for the people who write to you, taken from their address book entry or their organisation's logo.

Where pictures appear

Enable this option to show profile pictures in the inbox list.

This feature reaches into parts of Thunderbird that can change between releases. If the message list misbehaves, turn it off and report the problem.

Appearance

Initials are shown when no picture is found. A colour per sender makes them easier to tell apart; one grey keeps a long list calmer.

Privacy

Picture sources

Sources are tried from top to bottom, and the first picture found is used. Turning off sources you don't need makes lookups faster.
