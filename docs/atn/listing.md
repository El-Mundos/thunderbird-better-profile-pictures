## Name

Better Profile Pictures

## Summary (250 characters max)

Shows a picture for every sender: their photo, their organisation's logo, or
their initials. Fast on large folders, with full control over which online
sources are asked and what each one learns.

## Categories

Message and News Reading · Appearance

## Description

Better Profile Pictures puts a face next to your mail: in the message header
and, if you like, in the message list, in both the table and the cards view.

**Where the pictures come from**

1. Photos in your address books.
2. Online sources, in an order you choose: the brand logo an organisation
   publishes through BIMI, Gravatar photos, and DuckDuckGo site icons are on by
   default; Libravatar, Google, Icon Horse, Splitbee and a site favicon lookup
   can be switched on.
3. Initials in a colour derived from the address, when nothing is found.

**Built for large folders.** Only the rows on screen are looked up, each
sender once, with results cached and reused as you scroll.

**Your choice of what gets sent.** Online lookups send the sender's domain, or
a SHA-256 hash of their address, to the source being asked; never message
content and never your own address. Turn sources on and off one by one, or
pick a privacy mode:

- *Off*: every enabled source.
- *Balanced*: BIMI only, so no picture service learns who you correspond with.
- *Strict*: no network requests at all; address book photos and initials only.

**Per-sender rules.** Pin a specific picture for a sender or a whole domain,
or hide it.

**Also:** circle, rounded or square avatars; configurable cache lifetimes;
optional photos for newly created contacts.
